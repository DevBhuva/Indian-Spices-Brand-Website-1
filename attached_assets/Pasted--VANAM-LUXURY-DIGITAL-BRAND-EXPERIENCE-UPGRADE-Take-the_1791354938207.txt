# VANAM — LUXURY DIGITAL BRAND EXPERIENCE UPGRADE

Take the existing VANAM website specification and elevate it from a premium website into a **cinematic luxury digital brand experience**.

Do not redesign the brand concept.

Keep:

VANAM

"A Heritage of Indian Flavour"

Royal Indian spice house

Deep burgundy

Warm ivory

Antique gold

Deep forest green

Editorial typography

Indian heritage

Premium food photography

But significantly improve the visual storytelling, animation, interactions, transitions and overall perceived quality.

---

# 1. FIRST IMPRESSION

The website should feel expensive within the first 3 seconds.

The visitor should immediately understand:

VANAM = PREMIUM INDIAN SPICES

But the website should reveal the brand story gradually rather than presenting everything immediately.

The experience should feel like:

Luxury fashion website
+
Premium food brand
+
Indian heritage museum
+
Editorial magazine
+
Modern technology

---

# 2. CINEMATIC LOADING EXPERIENCE

Create a very short premium preloader.

Background:

Deep Burgundy

Center:

VANAM logo

Below:

A HERITAGE OF INDIAN FLAVOUR

Animation:

Logo slowly fades in.

A thin antique-gold line draws underneath.

A subtle grain texture moves in the background.

Then the logo fades away and the homepage hero reveals itself.

Duration:

Maximum 1.5–2 seconds.

IMPORTANT:

Never make the user wait unnecessarily.

On repeat visits, minimize or disable the preloader.
