# VANAM — PREMIUM INDIAN SPICE HOUSE

Create a premium, cinematic, multi-page brand website for an Indian spice brand called:

# VANAM

Position VANAM as a **royal Indian spice house** combining India's rich spice heritage with contemporary luxury.

This must NOT look like a generic grocery website, Shopify template, local masala website, or standard portfolio website.

It should feel like a sophisticated premium FMCG brand that could eventually compete visually with established Indian and international food brands.

# VANAM WEBSITE EXPERIENCE

The complete website should feel like entering:

"A modern Indian spice house with a centuries-old soul."

The visual hierarchy should be:

70% luxury editorial

20% Indian heritage

10% modern technology

Do not turn it into a colourful Indian festival website.

Keep it sophisticated.

Keep it restrained.

Keep it premium.

Keep it memorable.

---

# FINAL DESIGN TEST

Ask:

Would this website look appropriate on the packaging of a ₹1,000+ premium spice collection?

Would it look credible for international export?

Would a premium hotel chef take the brand seriously?

Would the website look good printed in a luxury food magazine?

Would the homepage still look beautiful without animations?

If the answer is yes, the design is successful.

Build VANAM as a **premium digital brand experience, not simply a website.**
